How to play

  1. Meet the daily three. At midnight a new main quest and two side quests deal out — picked from 21 missions across plastic, energy, water, food, transport and stuff.
  2. Do it for real, then tap Mark as done. The quests only count when real life happened — be honest, the planet takes notes.
  3. Build streaks, unlock badges. The main quest keeps your day alive; side quests stack bonus points. Streaks and points climb, badges pop out of the case with confetti.
  4. Everything stays on your device. Progress is saved in your browser’s localStorage — no account, no cloud, nothing leaves your machine.

Miss a day? The streak resets to zero — but your badges and total points are yours forever.

Start fresh?

This erases every quest, point, streak and badge saved in this browser. There is no undo — day one starts the moment you confirm.
