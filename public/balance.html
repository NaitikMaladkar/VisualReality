How it works

  1. Ten rounds, ten proposals. Each round deals one card — a power plant, a forest tract, a rail line, a marsh. Every one is modeled on real projects.
  2. Drag the card to a side — or tap it and tap a zone, or simply use the two buttons. Build grows the city meter (and usually costs nature); Protect grows nature (and usually costs growth).
  3. Watch the feedback. Both meters, the balance needle and the whole landscape — skyline, smog, river, trees, birds — react the moment a card lands.
  4. Reach the verdict. Keep both meters above 40 and within 25 points of each other after 10 rounds to unlock the Harmony ending.

There is no perfect card — only balanced portfolios. That’s the whole lesson.
