How we estimate — and where the numbers come from

Your answers are multiplied by published emission factors and shared fairly across the people in your home. It is an educational estimate, not an audit — but every factor is traceable:

  • Cars: 171 g/km petrol, 168 diesel, 114 hybrid, 53 electric — DEFRA GHG conversion factors.
  • Bus & rail: ~70 g/passenger-km (blend of DEFRA local bus and national rail).
  • Flights: 0.3 t per short-haul return, 1.6 t per long-haul return, economy with radiative forcing.
  • Electricity: 20 g green tariff, 250 low-carbon, 450 world-average, 750 coal-heavy g/kWh — IEA / Ember grid intensities.
  • Heating: household totals of 0.9 t heat pump to 3.0 t oil boiler, split per occupant.
  • Diets: 3.3 t heavy-meat to 1.5 t vegan per year — Poore & Nemecek, Science (2018) and Scarborough et al. (2014).
  • Comparisons: Our World in Data country averages (2023, rounded); the 2.0 t “fair share” reflects 1.5 °C pathways halving emissions by 2030.
